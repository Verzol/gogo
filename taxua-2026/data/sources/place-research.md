# Place introductions: research notes

Researched 14 October 2026. Original Vietnamese summaries live in `TRIP_DATA.placeGuides`; each introduction appears once in the travel story and has a small reading-source link. The detailed schedule stays in the original block fields and `itinerary.html`.

Research used the official Vietnam Tourism guide, reporting from VnExpress, Báo Tin tức, Báo Sơn La and Dân trí, plus local guides, travel operators, directories and firsthand visitor reports. Direct venue-owner documentation was not available for every café or restaurant. Venue histories, founding dates and unverifiable claims were therefore omitted. No prices, opening hours or performance dates from research replace the group schedule.

## Identity and uncertainty checks

- The ridge is the sightseeing ridge at Háng Đồng; descriptions do not borrow altitude or trekking history from the Trạm Tấu summit route.
- Cây Cô Đơn reporting mixes locations and distances. The copy describes the selected tree/river-valley view without inventing its age or claiming a tree species. Supporting Cùng Phượt search results identify the Suối Sập reservoir; the selected photograph also shows the river-valley setting.
- Lán Cô Đơn is a small photo stop, not Thào Cô Đơn café or Thào Coffee. The hut description is supported by the linked visitor photographs. [BAB Local’s itinerary](https://bablocal.com/post/goi-y-lich-trinh-du-lich-ta-xua-tu-tuc-2-ngay-1-dem-chi-tiet-tu-a-z?lang=en) corroborates the shared route.
- Hồ Mắt Ngọc sources are sparse. The copy limits itself to its lake-and-mountain setting; the group’s four-kilometre distance remains labelled as a group note. No lake age, construction story or name legend is inferred.
- Tháng 10’s lighting description is attributed to visitor reviews. Live music remains a group plan requiring confirmation; unrelated Mộc Châu fireworks notes are absent from the interactive story.
- Làng Văn Hóa reporting confirms the tourism stop. Café/nearby names and the 400 m walk come from the group schedule and are labelled accordingly; no heritage-village history is inferred.
- Khe Cải reporting documents increased interest since the 2025 cloud season; it does not establish a cloud guarantee for this trip.
- Bếp Củi sources conflict about a scenic view. The copy uses only its family-style food and rustic setting.
- Safety notes, optional visits, exact/null times, source rows, costs and illustrative-image captions remain available. `storyNote` trims display duplication while original notes stay in the full reading copy.
