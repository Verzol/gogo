Planning artwork only; this is not a screenshot or a runtime book component.
Requested model: `cx/gpt-image-2.5-sunburst`, high quality, PNG, 1536×1024.
Style references: existing painted wheel rim and cloud-coffee sticker. No crew
photographs or private diary content are sent to the image service.

## Exact prompt

Create ONE beautifully composed material-and-object concept study for a private
Vietnamese mountain-trip diary, matching the supplied watercolor artwork
references. This is a design study, not a website screenshot. Use hand-painted
watercolor and matte gouache, fine colored-pencil edges, natural paper fibers,
soft warm ivory highlights and restrained, believable shadows. Calm, intimate,
handmade and nostalgic, with the same friendly visual world as the references.

Landscape composition on a pale warm ivory artist-paper background. Show TWO
views of the SAME book with generous breathing room: a smaller closed book on
the left, and a much larger fully open two-page spread on the right. The open
spread should dominate. Keep all objects completely inside the canvas.

The closed book has worn deep indigo woven-cloth covers (#344866), a muted jade
spine (#365F64), fine warm ochre stitching and visibly layered cream page edges.
A small original mountain-and-cloud sketch is pressed into the lower part of
the cover. Leave the upper two thirds completely blank for a future live HTML
title. Add a narrow loose cotton closure ribbon, not a metal lock. The book is
a well-kept travel notebook, with light natural wear, not an ancient relic.

The large open book is viewed almost straight from above so its proportions and
two-page structure are clear. Warm ivory rag paper (#F3EAD4), softly irregular
outer edges, a convincing darkened center gutter, subtle binding threads and
small stacks of page edges underneath. Its left page is almost entirely blank
with extremely faint writing guidelines, one tiny pressed fern near the lower
outer corner, and space for a small portrait sticker near the upper outer
corner. Do not generate a face or portrait. Its right page has three completely
blank cream photo prints held by small pieces of translucent matte washi tape:
one larger horizontal print above two smaller prints, modest asymmetric tilts
of two to four degrees, and generous blank caption margins. The prints contain
no photographs, scenery or pictures; they are empty placeholders for real trip
photos. Preserve real room for readable content rather than filling the paper
with decoration. Add a very small indigo cloud doodle at the lower outer corner.

Seven tiny woven ribbon bookmarks emerge from the outer page edge, subtly
separated: indigo, golden yellow, fuchsia, raspberry, forest green, rust and
slate blue. They are accents, not large tabs or readable labels. A small painted
tea cup may sit outside the book, lower left, without overlapping either view.

Do not add any words, letters, numerals, handwriting, fake UI, names, labels,
watermarks, logos, people, faces, generated memories or real photographs.
No glossy 3D render, photorealistic leather, metallic gold frame, pirate-map
distress, burned edges, heavy stains, glowing effects or futuristic interface.
Both views must clearly belong to the same carefully made watercolor diary.
