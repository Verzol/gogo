```text
Create production-ready isolated watercolor game components for the Vietnamese Tà Xùa travel journal. The attached wheel rim and coffee sticker are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: borrow their matte watercolor/gouache texture, fine deep-teal pencil contours, cream cloud curls, layered jade mountains, restrained sunrise peach and orange. Do not reproduce the references themselves. Match their handcrafted storybook charm and legibility. Palette deep teal #173E48, indigo #101D35, jade #527E64, moss #789566, cream #F3E5C7, orange #EEA15A and peach #E8B299. Real pigment granulation INSIDE the objects, organic painted edges but precise functional geometry. Soft cream cut-paper edging where useful, never a broad white border. No plastic, photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy casino styling, neon, text, words, letters, logos or watermarks. Genuine alpha transparency outside the objects, NO white background, checkerboard or rectangular paper backdrop. Every component separate, complete and uncropped, with generous transparent gutters. No drop shadows floating outside the silhouette. No sample interface, hands or surrounding props.

OUTPUT: landscape 1536x1024 transparent PNG component sheet with EXACTLY THREE isolated objects. Top row: two identical-size perfectly circular coin faces, each about 540px diameter, centered at (400,360) and (1136,360). Bottom center: a separate horizontal thin ellipse representing the edge of the same coin, centered at (768,860), about 420px wide by 42px high. Keep at least 60px empty gutters. Both coin faces are perfectly front-on and geometrically circular with identical thin midnight-teal rim and warm cream inner ring; handmade texture must not distort their shape.
LEFT: SUN coin. A warm peach/ochre token, a simple sunrise-orange sun disc centered above a small layered jade mountain ridge along the bottom, two restrained cream cloud curls at the edges. Use a broad quiet sun disc, no spiky rays. RIGHT: CLOUD coin. Same token construction and diameter; a large soft cream cloud with a graceful spiral in the center, deep teal/blue sky inside the coin and a matching small mountain ridge at the bottom. Make the two faces unmistakably different at 64px. BOTTOM: EDGE with the same cream and teal outline, a few subtle ridges, no symbol. These are tactile painted wooden travel tokens, not actual money. No coin denominations or any text.
```
