# Drawing sticks and concealing sleeve

Exact prompt sent through 9Router with the existing wheel, coffee sticker and accepted coin sheet as image references.

```text
Create production-ready isolated watercolor game components for the Vietnamese Tà Xùa travel journal. The attached wheel rim and coffee sticker are STYLE REFERENCES ONLY: borrow their matte watercolor/gouache texture, fine deep-teal pencil contours, cream cloud curls, layered jade mountains, restrained sunrise peach and orange. Do not reproduce the references themselves. Match their handcrafted storybook charm and legibility. Palette deep teal #173E48, indigo #101D35, jade #527E64, moss #789566, cream #F3E5C7, orange #EEA15A and peach #E8B299. Real pigment granulation INSIDE the objects, organic painted edges but precise functional geometry. Soft cream cut-paper edging where useful, never a broad white border. No plastic, photorealism, 3D rendering, glossy casino styling, neon, text, words, letters, logos or watermarks. Genuine alpha transparency outside the objects, NO white background, checkerboard or rectangular paper backdrop. Every component separate, complete and uncropped, with generous transparent gutters. No drop shadows floating outside the silhouette. No sample interface, hands or surrounding props.

OUTPUT: portrait 1024x1536 transparent PNG component sheet. EXACTLY THREE isolated objects. Top area: TWO vertical drawing sticks, both front-on with identical width, identical rounded TOP tips and near-identical top-half wood grain. Left centered x=300: LONG stick from y=100 to y=900, width about 85px. Right centered x=720: SHORT stick from y=100 to y=720, width about 85px. Both have their tops at exactly the same height. The visible top halves must not give away which is short. Lower area: ONE opaque stick-concealing sleeve, centered x=512,y=1240, about 600px wide and 360px tall.
STICKS: warm light bamboo/wood watercolor, thin deep-teal pencil contours, cream highlights, restrained natural grain, no inscriptions or distinctive colored tip, no knots that imply a marked winner. Both complete and uncropped, both bottom ends visible, one clearly shorter only at the lower end. Maintain crisp functional silhouettes, do not bow or taper markedly.
SLEEVE: a small empty rectangular folded indigo/teal fabric holder, front view, closed bottom, quiet jade body, warm cream seam edges, a narrow band of tiny simple abstract woven mountain triangles across the bottom, short peach corner stitches. Its flat upper lip is clear and continuous, so a later interface can place the sticks behind it. No sticks inserted. No transparent holes in the front panel. No exact ethnic motif claims, no flowers, letters or extra objects.
The third reference is the approved sun/cloud coin sheet from this same game collection. Keep its palette, contour weight, pigment texture and mountain/cloud vocabulary consistent.
```
