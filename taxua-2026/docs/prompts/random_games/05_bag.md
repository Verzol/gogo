Genuine alpha transparency outside the objects, NO white background, checkerboard or rectangular paper backdrop. Every component separate, complete and uncropped, with generous transparent gutters. BOTTOM BALLS: three matching FRONT-ON circular wooden lottery tokens with slight watercolor shading, thin deep-teal outline, small cream highlight confined to the upper-left edge. First cream, second pale jade, third pale peach. Each has a large completely blank light center occupying 70% of its diameter for a real number or name to be added in HTML. No writing, symbols, numbers, eyes, or face. Same diameter, same material, equal visual prominence. No objects falling from the bags, no confetti or floating marks.
The third reference is the approved sun/cloud coin sheet from this same game collection. Keep its palette, contour weight, pigment texture and mountain/cloud vocabulary consistent.
