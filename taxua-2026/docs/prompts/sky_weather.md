# Sky and weather image prompts

Use each prompt independently to generate one image.

## 1. Left framing cloud — `cloud_hero_left.png`

```text
Create one isolated cloud cluster, taller and fuller on the left, tapering into two smaller billows and a slender right tip. Use unequal rounded lobes and an irregular underside, leaving the right side of the canvas mostly open.

Style: hand-painted watercolor and gouache for a Vietnamese mountain travel journal, with visible pigment granulation and fine, imperfect pencil contours. Warm cream #F3E5C7, subtle peach #E8B299 highlights from the upper left, pale blue-gray undersides and restrained teal #173E48 shadows. Airy, softly rounded volume with crisp painted edges.

Transparent PNG with genuine alpha, 2:1 landscape canvas, target 2048×1024 pixels. Keep the complete silhouette inside the canvas with transparent padding on every side.

Only the cloud: no sky, landscape, sun, stars, text, face, border, sticker outline, cast shadow, photographic or glossy 3D rendering, digital blur, checkerboard pattern or watermark.
```

## 3. Distant cloud sea — `cloud_sea_far.png`

```text
Create one isolated, wide, shallow sea of clouds seen from slightly above. Connect low overlapping billows into a gently raised center with irregular tapered ends. Suggest receding depth through subtle changes in tone. Avoid a straight baseline or rectangular fog bank.

Style: delicate hand-painted watercolor and gouache for a Vietnamese mountain travel journal. Pale cream #F3E5C7 and muted jade #527E64 diluted toward blue-gray, with faint peach #E8B299 light from the upper left. Restrained pigment granulation, faint imperfect pencil contours, low contrast and no digital blur.

Transparent PNG with genuine alpha, 3:1 landscape canvas, target 3072×1024 pixels. The complete cloud layer occupies about 80% of the canvas width and less than half its height, with transparent padding on every side.

Only the clouds: no sky, mountains, landscape horizon, sun, stars, text, face, border, sticker outline, cast shadow, photographic or glossy 3D rendering, checkerboard pattern or watermark.
```

## 4. Passing foreground cloud — `cloud_transition_near.png`

```text
Create one isolated, wide ribbon of connected cloud billows. Place the tallest cluster in the left third, a low dip in the middle and a smaller rounded rise near the right end. Give both tips a soft taper and vary the upper and lower contours. Keep the form airy, with distinct lobes rather than a solid white slab or straight baseline.

Style: hand-painted watercolor and gouache for a Vietnamese mountain travel journal, with visible pigment granulation, rounded volume and fine, imperfect pencil contours. Warm cream #F3E5C7, subtle peach #E8B299 highlights from the upper left, pale blue-gray undersides and restrained teal #173E48 shadows. Preserve detailed painted edges without digital blur.

Transparent PNG with genuine alpha, 3:1 landscape canvas, target 3072×1024 pixels. Keep the entire cloud and both tapered tips inside the canvas with transparent padding on every side.

Only the cloud: no sky, landscape, sun, stars, text, face, border, sticker outline, cast shadow, photographic or glossy 3D rendering, opaque haze gradient, checkerboard pattern or watermark.
```

## 5. Overcast cloud cluster — `cloud_overcast.png`

```text
Create one isolated, layered overcast cloud cluster. Concentrate rounded volume in unequal upper and side lobes, with shallow notches, an irregular underside and tapered ends. Suggest a cool, rainy mountain day with gentle muted tones, avoiding a nearly black cartoon storm cloud.

Style: hand-painted watercolor and gouache for a Vietnamese mountain travel journal, with visible pigment granulation and fine, imperfect pencil contours. Muted slate blue #386B80 and blue-gray, restrained teal #173E48 shadows, and soft cream #F3E5C7 highlights from the upper left. Keep the painted edges distinct without digital blur.

Transparent PNG with genuine alpha, 2:1 landscape canvas, target 2048×1024 pixels. Keep the complete silhouette inside the canvas with transparent padding on every side.

Only the cloud: no rain, snow, lightning, sky, landscape, sun, stars, text, face, border, sticker outline, cast shadow, photographic or glossy 3D rendering, checkerboard pattern or watermark.
```
