# Narrow paper frame, optional

Output: `map_paper_frame.png` · 3:2 · target 1536 × 1024

This frame is optional; the map can use a CSS border instead.

```text
Generate one thin, hollow handmade-paper frame for a watercolor travel journal, on a 3:2 landscape canvas targeting 1536 × 1024 pixels. Both the large central opening and the area outside the frame must have genuine alpha transparency.

Make a simple horizontal rectangular frame with gently uneven edges and softly rounded, imperfect corners. Use narrow warm-cream paper strips, approximately 3% of the canvas height in thickness. Leave a small transparent margin outside the complete frame. Keep the central opening broad, clean and entirely empty.

Use warm ivory paper with a soft cream base, tactile cotton fibers, faint watercolor granulation and a few pale-ochre variations within the strips. Add only a trace of muted teal pigment along occasional outer-edge sections. Keep the frame light, quiet and nearly flat, with consistent thickness and no heavy folds or curled corners.

Preserve clean inner edges so the frame can surround a detailed map without covering its content. No paper filling the center, map, scenery, illustrations, writing, grid, tape, ornaments, compass, border symbols, cast shadow, dark vignette, white background, checkerboard pattern or 3D mockup. Export a single PNG containing only the narrow paper frame and transparent empty areas.
```
